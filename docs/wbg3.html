What the B2 file audit supports

The inspected D2.8 raster has 44,090 positive pixels; B1 retains 40,199 after 3,891 catalogue-near pixels are removed; B2 retains 37,654 after a further 2,545 pixels are removed. B2 is an exact subset of its parent: 6,436 pixels removed, all within 200 m of the public catalogue, zero additions.

The official distance-weighted Tversky metric can reward removing predictions that receive little hidden-truth credit, if they otherwise contribute false-positive cost. But hidden faults may also occur near mapped faults. The hidden TPw, FPw, FNw, and exact evaluated upload are unavailable. The raster ancestry therefore does not prove that pruning caused the owner-reported change from 0.2600 to 0.2778. The 0.0178 difference is arithmetic on two unverified reports, not an organizer-confirmed paired delta.
