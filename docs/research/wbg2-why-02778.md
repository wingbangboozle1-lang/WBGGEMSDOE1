# GEMSDOE32 B2: what the raster audit shows—and what it cannot prove

**Decision:** the supplied 0.2778 is **OWNER-REPORTED / NOT ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**. Raster ancestry supports a catalogue-proximity pruning hypothesis, but the hidden labels and an artifact-linked submission receipt are unavailable. The evidence does not identify the causal reason for that value or validate a new score forecast. WBG-2 is **DOWNLOAD: YES (research only), SUBMIT: NO**; see the [current protocol review](../protocol-review-20261027.md) and [nine-site session review](../session-review-20261027.md).

## Direct answer: why might B2 have ranked highest?

The exact-file audit identifies the GEMSDOE32 B2 prediction set as a strict subset of the D2.8 parent: **6,436 positive pixels removed, zero added**. Every removed pixel is within 200 m of the mapped catalogue, and the final B2 set contains 37,654 pixels. The adjacent GEMSDOE31 B1 field contains 40,199 pixels; B2 removes a further 2,545 of those pixels. These are measured pixel-set relations, not verified submission scores.

A plausible explanation is precision cleanup: if some removed catalogue-adjacent guesses receive little or no credit against the unseen new-fault labels, deleting them can reduce the metric's weighted false-positive contribution. This is only a hypothesis. Those deleted pixels could include locations near unobserved true faults, and the hidden test labels are not available. The user-provided values (including 0.2708 and 0.2778) lack artifact-linked submission receipts here. Thus the observed raster edit and the reported score difference cannot be treated as a controlled causal experiment.

The full machine-readable ancestry and limitations are in [`evidence/h33_b2_forensics.json`](../../evidence/h33_b2_forensics.json). The focus-site table with original immutable artifact links is in the [executive summary](../executive-summary.html).

## Metric and interpretation boundary

The organizer's [problem and metric description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) describes a distance-weighted Tversky-style score. The repository's shared evaluator and its tests provide local implementations/checks; this session did not freshly retrieve the DrivenData page because that host is outside the available outbound allowlist. A metric that rewards spatial hits and penalizes false positives makes pruning a plausible candidate mechanism, but it does **not** prove that B2's removed pixels were false positives.

All nine focus-site leaders are binary dot fields, and the reported values co-vary descriptively with the fraction of dots near the mapped catalogue. This is a small, highly related set: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical decoded positive-pixel sets, and several other fields are nested pruning variants. The association is not independent evidence, a causal estimate, or a validated model of the hidden leaderboard.

## Why this repository does not publish a WBG-2 score estimate

An earlier 9-point regression output of 0.2824 (with a narrow residual-only interval) is **withdrawn**. Its inputs were unverified owner-reported values; two records duplicate the same decoded raster; catalogue proximity and dot budget are confounded; and there is no untouched validation of transfer to a new field. It is not a score, not a HOLDOUT-DTI measurement, and must not guide slot selection. WBG-2 has no qualifying hide-and-recover `HOLDOUT-DTI`, evaluator version, withheld-positive count, or 95% CI. Its DTI against the *full mapped catalogue* is a proxy check against the wrong target, not a validation result.

The recorded WBG-2 comparison against the nine requested focus artifacts is scoped, not global. Its broader literal registry audit records **100% forward 3 px overlap** with the WBG-C1 dense research surface, exceeding the protocol's 70% STOP threshold. The registry protocol offers no enrichment or density exemption, and the required pre-placement surface test was not measured. Therefore WBG-2 is **not cleared to submit**, even though its local TIFF format is valid and it differs from the nine focus-site files.

There is no defensible evidence here that a unique new raster will beat 0.2778, much less the user's cited 0.3195 leaderboard value. Answer: **unknown, not demonstrated**. A higher score requires a permitted method lane, a correctly constructed spatially blocked hide-and-recover comparison against the same-instrument best, independent leakage checks, both literal registry gates, and a separate authorized selector. The official competition data page is login-gated in the project record; input origin and access remain limitations.

## Irregularities to retain

- The reported historical 0.2778 has no submission-page receipt in this repository.
- The current user brief reports a leaderboard high of 0.3195. Stored repository observations include 0.4437 (2026-10-26) and 0.4544 (2026-10-27); current value is unresolved because no fresh official-page check was available.
- GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 are not independent prediction fields.
- WBG-2's nine-site uniqueness audit is not a global registry pass; the literal broader overlap gate fails.
- The four-step WBG-2 catalogue buffer is a raster-dilation operation, not proof of a Euclidean 400 m exclusion disk. Do not restate a Euclidean distance guarantee.
- A fault map or fault score is not direct confirmation of a geothermal vent or resource.
