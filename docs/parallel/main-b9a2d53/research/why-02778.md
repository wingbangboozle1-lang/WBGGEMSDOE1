## Metric identity

DTI = TP / (TP + 0.2 FP + 0.8 FN). Catalogue pixels are not in G. Deleting a catalogue-adjacent false positive lowers FP and barely touches TP → DTI rises. That is the B2 step.

## Unique-file ceiling

Protocol uniqueness vs this dense family requires ≥30% of dots >3 px from the network. This session’s MVGZ file has only 23–26% of dots within 3 px of any nine-site best — unique, and therefore mostly in the off-network strata GEMSDOE32’s live ledger called near-worthless.
