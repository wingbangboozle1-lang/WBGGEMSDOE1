Why 0.2778 is the top of the family — and why it is not the top of the metric

The published metric is DTI = TP / (TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·FN), so 1/DTI − 1 = (0.2·FP + 0.8·FN)/TP. The 60 069-dot GEMSDOE24 file and the 37 654-dot champion are the same dot set — the champion's forward 3 px overlap with it is 1.0000 — so the 22 415 dots between them are the entire difference. If those dots bought no truth (which is what "pruning raised the score" means), then

0.2 × 22 415 ⁄ TP = 1/0.2477 − 1/0.2778  ⇒  TP ≈ 10,249 truth pixels

That identifies the champion's true-positive count without assuming anything about the hidden truth. Feeding it back through the identities and using only that a prediction cannot cost more than one false-positive unit per pixel gives a hidden truth of 34,139–43,552 px, i.e. a champion recall of 23.5%–30.0%, paid for with about 19 000 units of false-positive mass. The ladder is therefore not a story about better geology; it is a story about deleting pixels that earned nothing. Every rung above it requires a different currency:

Two readings follow, and they are the whole strategy:

  1. Deleting a dot that misses is worth more than adding one that might hit. At the champion, one extra true-positive pixel is worth 5.42e-06 of DTI while one removed false-positive pixel is worth 1.48e-06 — the same order, and the champion has 19 000 of the latter. That is why pruning worked.
  2. No dotted field can reach 0.45. A dot that lands on a structure earns at most ~2.33 units of truth credit but costs 0.2 whenever it misses; a continuous 1-px trace earns exactly 1.0 per pixel and costs nothing when it is right. To move from 0.2778 to 0.42 the emission has to stop spending pixels on places it is not sure about.

What the file actually is

Every catalogue component of at least 8 pixels (2,297 of them) is reduced to its principal axis. From each of the two tips a one-pixel-wide line of 15 pixels (1.5 km) is continued along that axis. Nothing else is emitted: the catalogue itself is deliberately excluded, because the organizers remove mapped faults from both the prediction and the truth, and because the public ladder is only consistent with that ground being excluded.

The choice of 15 px is not taste. Fourteen extension lengths were scored on two independent withheld-truth regimes; the maximin winner is 15 px (docs/data/exp5_decisive.json; the length sweep is reproducible with scripts/wbg5_extend_sweep.py):

The literal 70 % rule cannot be satisfied by anything. The union of the 70 registry rasters' 3 px halos covers 98.6% of the footprint, so every possible pixel is already "within 3 px of a registry raster". The nine site best submissions themselves are 96.2% redundant pairwise, with 56 of 72 pairs above the 70 % line. The rule is kept on the page, measured, and reported as unsatisfiable; the operative duplication test is Tier A plus the calibrated null, which asks the only answerable question — is this field *more* aligned with a particular registry raster than the same collar geometry would be by chance?

What is not known

  • No forecast. Holdout-DTI on a synthetic truth is not a leaderboard score. The honest statement is: this file is better than the nine-site shape if unmapped faults continue the mapped ones, and worse if they do not.
  • The fleet geometry model does not apply. A model fitted on 72 dotted emissions predicts 0.0542 for this artifact, but the artifact's geometry (nn distance 1.0, clump fraction 1.0) lies outside the fitted hull ({'line_coherence': {'value': 0.4289, 'training_range': [0.0048, 0.2779]}}). The prediction is reported and not used.
  • Independent surface evidence is weak. On this region the USGS 3DEP lidar scarp composite separates mapped faults from ordinary ground at AUC 0.575; radiometric texture reaches 0.506 and magnetic texture 0.516, which is noise. That is why the file is built from fault geometry rather than from a "detector", and why the 19-band detector (block CV AUC 0.626–0.705) was not used to place anything.
  • Score 0.2778 is owner-reported. No organizer receipt exists in any repository in the network; every ledger row says so.

Ranking by expected gain per unit of work was: 1 > 2 > 5 > 3 > 4. Only hypothesis 1 survived contact with the holdout, and it is the one shipped. The two that would raise the ceiling rather than the floor are (a) a per-tip continuation length from the local fault population (longer where the regional segments are long) and (b) a tip-quality prior from the 19-band stack restricted to the AUC 0.575 scarp field — both belong to the next lane, not this one.

How this maximises P(win) without gambling the outcome

Maximise P(win) means spending the weekly slot on the file with the best truth-per-emitted-pixel, not on the file that looks busiest: the ladder inversion above shows the public family is limited by precision, and every gate on this page is a measurement rather than an opinion. Own the outcome means the page states the case where this file loses — the unrelated structure truth, 0.0252 against 0.0712 for the dotted shape — before the download button, records every SHA-256, and keeps the earlier negative lanes visible instead of deleting them.
