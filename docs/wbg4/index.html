03 / SCIENTIFIC DIAGNOSIS & SISTER-FLEET UNIQUENESS

Where WBG-4 advances the repo — and why it stops honestly

EMPIRICAL ADVANCES

Beats WBG-2, WBG-3 & WBG-H1; first untouched confirmation

On identical leak-free gems-segment-pooled-v1 (n=60,988), WBG-4 CSJF-1 (0.165424) outperforms WBG-2 (0.000341), WBG-3 SPI-PMR (0.118180), WBG-H1 (0.123310), and WBG-3 EXP3DET (0.138233), and achieves 0.079967 on blind off-catalogue USGS SGMC faults (d ≥ 300 m, n=63,121) without ever using SGMC in features or training. Critically, Arm 3 was frozen before touching seed=77, where it more than doubled the 19-band baseline (0.103660 vs 0.051234).

WHY WBG-4 TRAILS WBG-S2

Linear Horvitz-Thompson vs power-law crest sharpening

WBG-4 trails WBG-S2 (0.183594 / 0.147773) for two exact reasons: (1) WBG-4's linear Horvitz-Thompson posterior p_cal * (1 - q_self) preserves broader 5×5 scarp-apron mass than WBG-S2's p^1.65 crest sharpening + de-halo operator, which matters strongly when gems57 hides only 20% of quadrant faults (~2,830 truth px vs ~8,900 quadrant dots); and (2) WBG-S2 EXP-2 trained with USGS SGMC auxiliary fault labels (w=0.32), whereas WBG-4 kept SGMC 100% blind. Per Protocol Rule 6 (3-experiment cap), no post-hoc retuning was performed.
