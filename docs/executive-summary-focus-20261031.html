5. Irregularities flagged for review

  • Leaderboard figures conflict. The brief cites 0.4413 as the current high and 0.3195 as the highest score. The official leaderboard page read on 31 Oct 2026 shows #1 = 0.4544 and #50 = 0.3195, the last row shown. 0.3195 is therefore the rank-50 cutoff, not the top score.
  • Identical bytes appear under several sites and scores. 15 SHA-256 groups occur in more than one focus repository. Example: the 0.26 file 'dotted-h19-5-d2-8' is byte-identical under GEMSDOE24, GEMSDOE25, GEMSDOE30 and GEMSDOE32. Only one of those site labels can correspond to a leaderboard entry.
  • Dot families are not unique. All nine headline files share one dot family: every headline file has at least 0.87 of its dots within 3 px of every other headline file's dots (3-px overlap, shared definition vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py).
  • GEMSDOE32 B2 is a strict subset of its parent. B2 has 37654 dots, all inside the 44090-dot D2.8 parent; 6436 parent dots were deleted, at 1-2 px from the public catalogue.
  • The proxy does not reproduce the reported ranking. Spearman rho = 0.0572 (p = 0.8054, n = 21). The proxy also scores B2 -0.0368 below its parent, the opposite sign to the reported +0.0178 gain. Neither the B2 explanation nor any proxy estimate can be confirmed.
  • The literal 70% overlap gate cannot be passed by any dot field. The r13 lattice (206,895 dots) matches 99.79% of the new file's dots, 99.91% of B2's, and 99.87% of a uniform random field at the same density. This is a gate defect that the owner must decide on, not a property of the candidate.
  • Inherited holdout measures the wrong population. The competition target is faults that are NOT in the public USGS database (official problem page). The holdout withholds catalogue segments and rewards placement tight to visible traces (vendor/gems57/offcatalogue.py docstring). Its numbers are not used here.
  • Proxy layer is approximate. The raw SGMC raster is not in this sandbox (mrdata.usgs.gov is not on the allowlist). The derived layer is a ~1 px dilation of SGMC and still contains catalogue pixels (IR-57-SGMC-01); the proxy removes them.
  • Training features and organizer data are unavailable. The DrivenData data page requires login; training_features.tif is not in this checkout. No model was trained here.

6. Limitations and what is needed

  • DrivenData data and submission pages require login. Not accessible from this sandbox. Needed: a human or the owner to supply official data and any organizer receipt.
  • Owner decision on gate G4. Literal 70% is saturated by every dot field. Needed: a written rule, for example the density-matched lift in the repo.
  • Raw SGMC from USGS mrdata. Needed: allowlist access or owner-supplied copy.
  • Full 469-raster registry was not re-fetched. Uniqueness was measured against the 9 focus repositories (466 prediction rasters on grid) only.
