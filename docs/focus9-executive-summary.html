GEMSDOE32 B2: what does the reported 0.2778 establish?

Measured raster ancestry — not score causation

The pinned rasters establish a deletion-only lineage on the 100 m UTM 11N grid:

  • D2.8: 44,090 positive pixels.
  • B1: 40,199 pixels; exactly 3,891 D2.8 pixels removed within one 100 m pixel of the public catalogue.
  • B2: 37,654 pixels; a further 2,545 removed from the 100–200 m band. Relative to D2.8, B2 removes 6,436 pixels within 200 m and adds none.
  • GEMSDOE36 contains all B2 pixels plus 1,200 additions. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 decode to identical D2.8 prediction pixels.

These are pixel-set relations, not proof of the exact bytes submitted or of hidden-label contributions. See the B2 raster audit and nine-site audit.

Metric interpretation — causal effect unavailable

The official metric description specifies distance-weighted Tversky scoring, with a 300 m triangular distance kernel and asymmetric FP/FN weights. For a fixed hidden truth, changing predictions can affect weighted true-positive, false-positive, and false-negative contributions.

  • Deleting catalogue-near pixels could lower weighted false-positive cost, but nearby hidden faults could also mean lost true-positive credit or additional false-negative cost. Those terms cannot be recovered from the public rasters.
  • The values 0.2600 (D2.8) and 0.2778 (B2) are both USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED. Their 0.0178 difference is arithmetic on supplied claims, not an organizer-confirmed paired delta.
  • The available evidence does not show that pruning caused the reported difference. It establishes neither a score ceiling near 0.28 nor a fixed increase in weighted true positives needed to reach another score.

Conclusion: the raster lineage is measured; the reason for the reported 0.2778 is unknown without authenticated artifact-linked receipts and the hidden-label score components.

    Measure scale-dependent displacement of gravity and magnetic gradient maxima relative to detrended-elevation gradients.   3. Rank 3 — Well heat-flow residuals with spatial sampling controls
    Test uncertainty-aware residuals from background conductive heat flow alongside gravity/magnetic gradients, only where well metadata and coverage support it.   4. Rank 4 — INGENIOUS shallow temperature-probe residuals
    After spatially cross-fitted seasonal/elevation adjustment, compare probe residuals and local relief with potential-field edges. Mimics: soil moisture, vegetation, sun/shade, season, and conductive lithology. How to turn a validated file into a competition submission (without a human inspector)

A pass is necessary but not sufficient.
  2. Check uniqueness: Run .venv/bin/python scripts/audit_registry.py --candidate docs/downloads/<your-file>.tif. Require |ρ| ≤0.90 and (competing-method) dot overlap ≤70% at 3 px. If the file is dotted, literal r13 lattice saturation currently blocks promotion — await registry policy update.

Verify holdout victory: .venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate_holdout.py --candidate <method> must use a frozen same-evaluator current-best comparator and appropriate paired uncertainty before any slot is considered.
