## 2. Locked Three-Arm Experimental Design (`<= 3` Experiments)

All three arms share the **exact same** spatially blocked splits, pixel-exact visible-fault masking, off-catalogue emission collar (`EDT(~visible) > 2.0 px`), dot budget (`36,800` binary `{0.0, 1.0}` dots across the `5,167,373`-pixel footprint, `0.7122%` density), and shared `emit_celf_submodular` 300 m triangular-kernel emitter (`min_separation_px = 2.25`):

1. **Arm 1 (`WBG3-CTRL-19B` — Baseline Control):**
   - **Inputs:** Only the 19 official cached competition bands (`data/template57/`, SHA-256 verified against `registry/template_pins.json`).
   - **Learner:** Spatially blocked Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (`LOQO`) `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`max_iter=140, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=80, learning_rate=0.06, l2_regularization=4.0`, positive sample weight balancing sampled negatives `max_neg=180,000` at `EDT(~visible) >= 4 px`).
   - **Emission:** Single-regime 300 m CELF submodular max-cover (`36,800` binary dots, `EDT(~visible) > 2.0 px`).
2. **Arm 2 (`WBG3-SPI-PMR-PC` — Pure Label-Free Multi-Physics + Monogenic Phase Congruency, 36 Channels):**
   - **Inputs:** 19 official bands + 5 `SPI` local-wavenumber channels + 4 `PPDR` Poisson magnetite-destruction/radiometric channels + 5 2D FFT Riesz monogenic phase-congruency channels + 3 label-free GDR 1391 hydrothermal conduit channels (`36` static channels total; **zero** 1 m LiDAR channels and **zero** catalogue-derived channels).
   - **Learner:** Fold-safe regional elevation/coordinate residualizer (`Ridge(alpha=250.0)` fit strictly on training background) + dual-depth `LOQO` `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` ensemble (`depth-A`: `max_leaf_nodes=31, lr=0.06, max_iter=160`; `depth-B`: `max_leaf_nodes=63, lr=0.05, max_iter=160, l2_regularization=6.0`).
   - **Emission:** Single-regime 300 m CELF submodular max-cover (`36,800` binary dots, `EDT(~visible) > 2.0 px`).
3. **Arm 3 (`WBG3-SPI-PMR-WCS` — Preregistered Primary Candidate, 40 Channels):**
   - **Inputs:** Arm 2's 36 label-free multi-physics channels + 4 fold-safe `fold["visible"]`-only structural mechanics channels (`wcs_wing_crack_lobe`, `wcs_relay_stepover`, `wcs_echelon_halo`, `wcs_orphan_hydro_boost`), computed strictly from `fold["visible"]` inside every fold (and from `labels.tif` only for full-map inference after OOF validation).
   - **Fusion & Emission:** Non-negative corroborative log-odds structural relay boost (`delta_logit = 0.55 * wcs_wing_crack_lobe + 0.65 * wcs_relay_stepover + 0.35 * wcs_echelon_halo + 0.50 * wcs_orphan_hydro_boost`, gated by subsurface/monogenic support so bare geometric halos without physical anomalies are suppressed) + **Two-Regime 300 m CELF Submodular Allocation**:
     - `Regime A` (`27,600` dots, `75%`): Crustal `SPI` + Monogenic Phase-Congruent piedmont/intrabasin fault ridges;
     - `Regime B` (`9,200` dots, `25%`, initialized with `Regime A`'s exact 300 m kernel coverage grid so zero 300 m disks are duplicated!): Concealed magnetite-destroyed hydrothermal conduits + crack-tip wing-crack / relay-ramp step-overs (`36,800` binary dots total).
