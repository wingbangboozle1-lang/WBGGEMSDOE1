What was actually tested?

WBG-C1 · Hessian non-coaxiality

From literal bands cond_surf, depth_to_base_surf and geod_dilaterate, take scale-normalized Gaussian Hessians at 300 m and 800 m. The normalized matrix commutator detects disagreement in principal curvature directions. Require curvature strength and cross-scale persistence; supply positive-dilatation support as a separate feature.

C(A, B) = |[A B − B A]₀₁| / (‖A‖F ‖B‖F)
C = 0 for commuting / shared-axis tensors
C ∈ [0, 1] for the real symmetric 2 × 2 tensors used here

Named mimic: oblique saline-clay basin fill, lithologic contacts or shared inversion artifacts can produce the same signature. The operator itself is an unproven research hypothesis.

Four candidates ranked before implementation

1 · Conductivity/depth Hessian commutator — low cost, highest-priority conjecture

Layers: literal bands 17, 15, 8. Signature: multiscale non-coaxial curvature, not scalar gradient strength, Euler depth or inherited ridge thinning. Missing-catalogue rationale: concealed geometric complexity without catalogue proximity. Mimic: oblique basin-fill/lithologic boundaries. Existing shared cache is sufficient; no external data needed. Exact operator not found in inspected code, but global/unpublished novelty is not established.

Executed once; negative. Expected improvement was qualitative and uncertain, never a numeric forecast.

2 · Depth-conditioned magnetic texture transport — medium cost, untested

Layers: cached RTP, TMI and depth proxy. Signature: lagged texture changes along versus across depth-contour directions. It may flag covered offsets without an exposed scarp; volcanic/intrusive contacts or survey leveling can mimic it. More specific than a high-amplitude magnetic edge. Related peer texture/phase proposals already exist; exact added information and lane novelty are unknown. No new source needed for an initial screen.

3 · Radiometric compositional changes across saddles — medium cost, conditional

Layers: USGS GeoDAWN K/Th/U and terrain saddles. Signature: tangent-coherent cross-saddle compositional changes, not raw counts. Fracture-controlled alteration might lie outside mapped traces; sorting, weathering and lithology can mimic it. Ratio features already exist in peer code, so a ratio alone is not novel. The official GeoDAWN catalogue/license was checked, and owner-derived layers exist; original bytes, quantization meanings and coverage must be independently established before calling this ready.

Official free USGS source · DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ ↗

4 · Anisotropic conductivity-boundary persistence — medium cost, untested

Layers: cached conductivity and depth proxies. Signature: boundary survival under smoothing along, but not across, a local orientation tensor. It might retain elongated concealed discontinuities; saline deposits or gridding seams are mimics. Different proposed statistic from a scalar edge, but related coherence/vesselness already exists. Needs a future assigned lane and frozen validation, not an automatic slot.

Grounded in literature, not validated by analogy
