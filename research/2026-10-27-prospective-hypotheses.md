[USGS Siler (2023)](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/structural-discontinuities-and-their-control-hydrothermal-systems-great-basin-usa) associates fault intersections/terminations with hydrothermal circulation. [USGS Faulds et al. (2019)](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and) demonstrates that a promising blind geothermal target required *converging* structural, geophysical, temperature, and drilling evidence. **Fault probability is not a confirmed vent or reservoir.** These sources motivate tests; they do not validate the proposed operators.
