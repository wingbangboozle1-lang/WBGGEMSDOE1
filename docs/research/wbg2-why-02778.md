# Why the reported GEMSDOE32 result cannot yet be explained causally

The supplied **0.2778 is USER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED**: no organizer submission-page receipt in this repository ties it to the raster. The latest [GEMSDOE32 page](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html) also describes it as an owner report without a file-specific receipt.

The [nine-site pixel audit](../data/nine-site-audit.json) shows a nested family: D2.8 contains 44,090 dots; B1 retains 40,199 after pruning within one pixel of the known catalogue; B2 retains 37,654 after further pruning within two pixels. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have the same decoded dot field; GEMSDOE36 contains every B2 dot and 1,200 more. Different file hashes are not independent methods.

The [official metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) has a 300 m triangular kernel and α=0.2, β=0.8. Reducing low-credit or redundant positive mass **could** help the numerator/denominator tradeoff, but the hidden new faults can be near known faults too. No hidden labels are available here; the causal benefit of those removals, and whether any new candidate can surpass the reported figure, is unknown. Do not infer a universal catalogue exclusion radius.

The earlier WBG-2 regression used repeated, related, unreceipted historical claims and extrapolated outside the fitted budget range. Its residual-based interval was not a validated prediction interval. It is **withdrawn**, not a leaderboard score or a credible comparison to 0.2778. Earlier synthetic and catalogue-only numbers cannot substitute for a visible-only, whole-segment HOLDOUT-DTI with a CI.

The current [executive summary](../wbg2.html) reports the actual negative registry stop and local validation. A blind geothermal system requires convergent structural, thermal, geophysical and other evidence; see the [USGS play-fairway study](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and). A fault prediction is not proof of a vent or geothermal resource.
