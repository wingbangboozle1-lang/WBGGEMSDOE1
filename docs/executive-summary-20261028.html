## Why the GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 0.2778 is the historical leader

B2 added no geological detections. It removed exactly 6,436 parent dots, all within 200 m of the public catalogue. If those dots had zero hidden-truth credit, deleting them reduces the weighted false-positive denominator and raises DTI; this is a plausible precision-cleanup mechanism.
