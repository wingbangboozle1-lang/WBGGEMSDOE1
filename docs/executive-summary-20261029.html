4 · Why GEMSDOE32 B2 scored 0.2778 (verified mechanism, user-reported scores)

StepFile familyPositivesRemovedUSER-REPORTEDWithin 300 m of catalogue

B2GEMSDOE32 · 0.2778 file37,6542,545 more within 200 m (≤2 px); measured 2,545 at ≤2 px in B10.27785.8% (0 within 200 m)

Verified from the raster bytes: B2 has zero dots within 200 m of the catalogue. Its 2,171 near-catalogue dots lie between 200 m and 300 m from the catalogue. D2.8 → B1 → B2 is a pure removal ladder: B2 ⊂ B1 ⊂ D2.8, and GEMSDOE36 = B2 + 1,200 dots. The user-reported lineage rises +0.0108 then +0.0070 with each removal.

Mechanism (plausible, not proven): under the official metric (α = 0.2 for false positives, β = 0.8 for false negatives, 300 m triangular kernel), a dot near a known mapped fault is charged as a false positive unless a hidden new fault lies within the kernel. Removing dots that carry no hidden-fault credit lowers the false-positive term at little cost to true-positive credit. This is the "precision by exclusion" explanation, consistent with the lineage. It is not a causal proof: hidden labels are unavailable and the scores are user-reported.

Descriptive pattern (n = 9 distinct positive sets): Spearman ρ = -0.93, p ≈ 0.0002 between the share of dots within 300 m of the catalogue and the user-reported score. Fewer near-catalogue dots goes with a higher reported score. This is the strongest pattern we found, and it is the first hypothesis to test. The caveat is that the scores are unverified and the files come from one family.

Why you cannot submit a "better" pruned B2: any further pruning of B2 yields a subset of B2, so 100% of its dots overlap B2. That is a copy under the user's rule and a lane duplicate under the protocol.

8b · How to submit, if a file is ever cleared

Today the answer is no. These steps apply only after a file has its own verified run card with a PASS verdict. None of the eleven files above qualifies.

  1. Choose the file by its run card, not by score. Its SHA-256 must match the run card, and its positive set must pass the lane test (≤70% of dots within 3 px of another named raster) and the exact-duplicate test.
  2. Validate locally: python scripts/validate_submission.py path/to/file.tif, then add --require-submit-ready. Required: single-band float32 GeoTIFF, EPSG:32611, 3730×3292, 100 m, values in [0, 1], and data outside the bounds as NaN (the official sample's convention).
  3. Name and note: a unique file name, plus a note of at most 140 characters that says what the file is.
  4. Disclose generative-AI use in the narrative, as the official rules require.
  5. Upload only through the DrivenData portal. The rules allow one final submission and three per week. Do not upload from a script, and never enter or store DrivenData credentials here.
  6. Record the receipt. Only an organizer or portal receipt makes a score ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Until then every number is HOLDOUT-DTI (with its evaluator version, n, and CI), USER-REPORTED, or a projection, and a projection is never a score.

Duplicate note: data/bridge/labels.tif and data/bridge/existing_faults.tif are byte-identical (SHA-256 7ba308cc…, 425,830 bytes). Treat them as one file.
