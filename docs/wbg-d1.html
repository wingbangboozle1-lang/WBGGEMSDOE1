Why SUBMIT: NO despite a valid, unique file

  • No measured improvement. Adding seven radiometric channels to the 19-band gradient-boosted model changed pooled HOLDOUT-DTI from 0.014146 to 0.014072; paired Δ -0.000074 with 95% CI [-0.000280, 0.000112] — zero is inside the interval.
  • The deterministic radiometric detector underperformed random dots on catalogue recovery (EXP-1: 0.0283 vs 0.0345), because its physically motivated off-ridge placements target concealed basin structures that the local catalogue-truth proxy does not contain.
  • Literal registry STOP. The uniform synthetic r13-lattice calibration grid overlaps every sparse dot field by construction (pigeonhole, IR-WBG1-01), and the project's own continuous WBG-C1 surface is a universal overlap blocker. Against every real geological dot-field prior the lane is distinct (max overlap 0.4846).
  • Burning a weekly 3-entry slot on an unproven negative would reduce P(Win). The file is retained as a reproducible research deliverable and a registered negative result.
