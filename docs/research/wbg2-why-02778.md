# GEMSDOE32 B2: what is measured, and what is not

**USER-REPORTED / NOT ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED:** the supplied B2 value is 0.2778 and the related D2.8 parent value is 0.2600. We do not have artifact-linked organizer submission receipts for either value. The public leaderboard, even when accessible, is not such a receipt.

## Pixel evidence

The frozen pairwise forensic analysis, [`h33_b2_forensics.json`](../data/h33-b2-forensics.json), reports that B2 is an exact **deletion-only subset** of the D2.8 parent:

- 37,654 prediction pixels kept, 6,436 removed and none added.
- Every removed parent pixel is within 200 m of the public mapped catalogue on the 100 m grid.
- This establishes ancestry and proximity. It does not identify previously unknown faults or prove a causal score explanation.

For the initial competition metric, removing prediction weight that earns insufficient hidden-fault credit can lower the false-positive component of the DTI denominator. If the removed dots had little or no hidden-label credit, their removal could therefore improve the score. The hidden labels, credit terms, exact score receipts and controlled paired organizer experiment are unavailable; we **cannot** conclude that all removed dots were false positives, that the improvement was caused by the pruning, or that the same rule would improve a new TIFF. The inherited claim that each removed dot is automatically a pure false positive is withdrawn.

## WBG-2 correction

WBG-2 was a new density-prior placement, not an exact B2 copy. That is not enough for the user's novelty protocol. The shared exact-file audit measured 99.8972% forward 3-pixel overlap with a hash-pinned GEMSDOE24 lattice and 100% with the WBG-C1 registered continuous surface. The literal limit is 70%; **STOP**, without a density or chance-enrichment exception. This is a protocol failure, not proof that geology or pixels were copied. Its former nine-champion scan did not cover all registered rasters and its private validation omitted an exact transform check.

The former 0.2824 projection, purported confidence interval and claim of upload readiness are withdrawn. There is no qualifying visible-only buffered WBG-2 HOLDOUT-DTI, and no validated leaderboard prediction. See the [corrected machine-readable run card](../data/wbg2-review.json) and [executive audit](../wbg2.html).
