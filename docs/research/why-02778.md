## Cautious metric interpretation

The [official competition page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) describes a distance-weighted Tversky metric with a 300 m triangular kernel and weights `α = 0.2`, `β = 0.8`:

```text
DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2 × FP_w + 0.8 × FN_w)
```

Conditionally, if some removed pixels had little or no credit against hidden faults and contributed false-positive cost, removing them **could** improve DTI. But a hidden fault can also lie near a mapped fault. Without hidden labels and the artifact-linked organizer receipt, the actual `TP_w`, `FP_w`, `FN_w`, and evaluated file are unknown. The proposed precision-cleanup explanation is therefore a **plausible mechanism hypothesis, not a finding of causation**.

A 200 m collar is a measured property of this raster, not an organizer rule or universal geological principle. This audit does not establish that all catalogue-adjacent predictions are false positives or that further deletion would continue to help.
