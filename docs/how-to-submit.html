What automated preflight checks

  • One float32 raster band, values in [0,1] and no NaN/inf anywhere under our conservative export policy.
  • CRS EPSG:32611, shape 3,730 rows × 3,292 columns, 100 m pixel grid and exact reference geotransform/bounds.
  • Zero support outside the mirrored footprint and on mapped catalogue pixels.
  • On-disk roundtrip plus a ZIP containing exactly one identical GeoTIFF.
  • Separate scientific, leakage and registry gates; no “human inspection only” escape hatch.

The official format says data outside the bounds should be null/NaN. All-finite zeros outside the mirrored footprint are a conservative local project choice, not verified portal acceptance; the two descriptions may not refer to exactly the same area. We cannot prove the cause of the old rejected upload without its actual bytes and receipt.
