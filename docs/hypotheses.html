Rank #1 — SPI-PMR-WCS-1: Crustal Source-Parameter Imaging × Monogenic Phase × Wing-Crack Synthesis EXECUTED · HOLDOUT-DTI 0.1182 (4/4 wins)

Exact layer(s) involved: Official 19-band stack bands rtp (14), tmi (16), iso_grav_anom (13), det_elev (12), elev (11), geod_shearstr (7), geod_dilaterate (8) + label-free OpenEI GDR 1391 thermal spring concessions (DOI:10.15121/1881483, fault-distance columns stripped) + fold-visible fault mask. Zero 1 m LiDAR, zero 10 m DEM.

Physical signature (transform): (1) Thurston & Smith (1997) local wavenumber kSPI=‖∇ atan2(∂zF,‖∇hF‖)‖ over shallow-upward-continued RTP/gravity — peaks directly over buried contact regardless of inclination; (2) Felsberg-Sommer 2D FFT Riesz monogenic phase congruency (dimensionless, amplitude-invariant) across degraded <1 m piedmont scarps; (3) Pollard-Segall tensile wing-crack lobes at ±32° from visible tip/step-over (visible-only).

Why it catches catalogue-missing faults: Intrabasinal Quaternary / blind geothermal faults under 10–250 m of Lake Lahontan/playa alluvium have no subaerial scarp, but Tertiary volcanic vs basin-fill juxtaposition gives sharp kSPI directly above the buried contact; degraded range-front splays retain across-scale phase alignment. Prior ridge-thinning family missed these because it followed exposed scarps only.

Named non-fault mimic: Pleistocene pluvial Lake Lahontan wave-cut benches, shallow buried basaltic flow/sill margins, and railroad/highway embankments — all low-amplitude contrast-invariant steps that mimic the above signatures.

How it differs from repo: Differs from WBG-C1 (Hessian commutator, continuous, no dots), TIP1 (fixed isotropic tip kernel), MVGZ (magnetic trough heuristic), WBG-H1/S2 (1 m LiDAR + 10 m DEM derivatives), and WBG-2 (0-feature random KDE). Expected vs measured: Expected highest gain — measured HOLDOUT-DTI 0.118180 (gems-segment-pooled-v1 n=60,988, CI [0.1037,0.1324], paired Δ +0.0274 vs 0.09078 ctrl, 4/4 folds won; gems57-pooled-hide-v2 0.121862 vs 0.07647). Cost: medium-high (40-channel FFT + Riesz + WCS). Source: Free official USGS GeoDAWN (DOI:10.5066/P93LGLVQ) + GDR 1391 thermal; both obtainable. Verdict: negative — passes competing-sparse (59.38% vs 206) but literal lattice STOP (99.99% vs r13) and trails S3DEP1 0.1661, so no promotion.

Rank #2 — PPDR-1: FFT Poisson Pseudogravity Magnetite-Destruction Residual Ablated in WBG-3 Arm 2 · +12.0% vs ctrl

Exact layer(s): Official rtp (14), iso_grav_anom (13), cond_surf (17) + USGS GeoDAWN radiometrics K,Th,U,TC (DOI:10.5066/P93LGLVQ — USGS GeoDAWN page).

Transform: Cordell-Taylor 2D FFT Poisson pseudogravity gpseudo=F−1[F(RTP)/max(‖k‖,k0)] → normalized gravity-gradient minus pseudogravity-gradient residual, gated by high K/Th and cond_surf.

Why catalogue-missing: Hydrothermal upwelling oxidizes magnetite to clay/pyrite — magnetic gradient collapses while structural density contrast persists; enriched K/Th metasomatism marks the alteration halo missed by scarp mapping.

Mimic: Felsic ash-flow calderas, evaporite playas (high K/Th without structure). Diff vs repo: SPI is local wavenumber; PPDR is integral residual — complementary. Peer ratio-only features do not implement Poisson integration. Cost: medium (FFT Poisson). Expected gain: moderate (+12.0% in Arm 2, 0.1017 vs 0.09078). Validation: Same holdout as Rank #1; leakage canary max AUC 0.57 <0.90. Source: Free official GeoDAWN; obtainable.

Rank #3 — AFBD-1: Thin-Plate Flexural Basement Curvature & MT Conductance Anisotropy Preregistered · Deferred for budget

Exact layer(s): Official-only: depth_to_base_surf (15), cond_10_to_20_km (2), cond_2_to_5_km (4), cond_surf (17), geod_shearstr (7), geod_dilaterate (8).

Transform: Elastic-plate biharmonic flexural hinge ∇4zb at hidden master normal-fault rollovers + strike-projected deep-to-shallow MT vertical conductance transfer — coincidence of flexure and MT vertical “pipe”.

Why catalogue-missing: Deep master faults with no surface scarp still flex basement and channel conductance vertically through gouge — no scarp needed.

Mimic: Differential compaction hinges over paleo-valleys. Diff vs repo: No prior repo lane used biharmonic flexure × MT conductance; WBG-3 used SPI/PPDR/phase but not flexure-MT. Cost: low-medium (no new data). Expected gain: moderate; needs untouched confirmation. Source: Already in 19-band stack; obtainable without external download.

Rank #4 — INSAR-1: NASA ASF OPERA Sentinel-1 DInSAR Secular Velocity-Gradient Discontinuity Deferred — sandbox network gate

Exact layer(s) & official free source: NASA/JPL OPERA Sentinel-1 L3 Displacement products resampled to 100 m UTM 11N (OPERA DAA C, ASF Search), computing strain-axis-projected LOS secular velocity gradient discontinuities across zero-relief playas.

Why catalogue-missing: Active intrabasinal faults creeping <1 mm/yr offset playa LOS velocity with no scarp — invisible to 1 km GPS interpolation but visible to 10 m InSAR.

Mimic: Seasonal groundwater pumping subsidence bowls. Diff vs repo: No prior repo lane used orbital radar — complements mag/grav/optical. Cost: high (OPERA staging, coregistration, seasonal unmixing). Expected gain: potentially highest on playa-masked blind faults. Gate: asf.alaska.edu outside sandbox allowlist — defer until primary OPERA granules are hash-pinned on unrestricted machine (bash scripts/download_competition_data.sh pattern). Verify availability via official OPERA page before counting as viable; currently not obtainable in sandbox.

Rank #5 — GEO-ALT: GDR-1391 Hydrothermal Synthesis + Relay-Ramp Orphan Boost (included in WBG-3 WCS-boost) Included as orphan-hydro boost · +5.2% vs WBG-2

Exact layer(s): OpenEI GDR 1391 geothermal wells/springs (fault-distance columns stripped; see IR-035) as orphan seeds, plus fold-visible relay-ramp geometry.

Transform: Visible-only relay step-over score × proximity to GDR-1391 2-m sinter/temperature probes — orphan hydrothermal anomalies near visible segment ends are boosted as proxies for through-going blind segments.

Why catalogue-missing: Blind geothermal up-flow may surface as sinter aprons or warm wells offset from mapped traces — orphan hydrothermal signals flag unmapped inter-segment relays.

Mimic: Anthropogenic geothermal production wells. Diff vs repo: Prior lanes used hydrothermal as standalone, not as visible-structural corroboration. Cost: low (point overlay). Expected gain: modest but robust on blind systems. Source: GDR 1391 free official (GDR 1391 → DOI:10.15121/1881483); obtainable, fault-distance columns must be stripped (verified canary AUC 0.5727).
