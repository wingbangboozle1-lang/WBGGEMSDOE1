Why GEMSDOE32 B2 scored 0.2778 — and can we beat it?

Mechanism (evidence-backed, PhD forensics, receipts in proxy_calibration.json and h33_b2_forensics.json):

  1. B2 is a deletion-only subset of D2.8. Exact pixel forensics: B2 = D2.8 minus 6,436 dots, all within 2 px (200 m) of the public catalogue, plus zero added dots. The 0.2600 → 0.2778 gain is precision cleanup, not new detections.
  2. The official metric makes catalogue-near dots pure loss. DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w) with TP_w saturating per truth pixel (max over dots × 300 m triangular kernel). Catalogue pixels are excluded from scoring, so a dot 1–2 px off a mapped trace can earn nothing but FP_w. Removing those dots raises DTI — exactly the observed +0.0178.
  3. The dotted representation matters more than the corridor source. The same corridor family as a continuous field (h19-5 powerlaw, 0.1922) scores 0.068 lower than its dotted budget form (d2.8, 0.2600): redundant corridor mass is FP_w under the max-coverage kernel.
  4. Family-wide rank structure (n=12, one family, OWNER-REPORTED). Score falls monotonically with catalogue proximity (Spearman −0.944, p=4.1e-6) and with SGMC off-catalogue coverage (−0.972, p=1.3e-7 — a family artifact of dot budget, not evidence that new-fault coverage hurts). Mechanism fit: score ≈ 0.2896 − 0.178·frac(within 3 px of catalogue).

Can we beat 0.2778? Plausible, not proven. Our field applies the validated mechanism completely (strict 3 px catalogue halo — frac3 = 0.000, the first member of the family to do so) with placement that is scientifically distinct and measurably better on the independent off-catalogue instrument: PROXY-DTI 0.0972 vs B2 0.0913 (+6.4% relative) on faults present in the state compilation but absent from the competition catalogue. The fold-safe holdout shows the placement beats a same-budget random control with a paired CI excluding zero (Δ +0.0101, 95% CI [+0.0011, +0.0180]). Calibrated estimates: 0.275 (proxy fit) to 0.290 (mechanism fit) — i.e. at or modestly above 0.2778. The dominant uncertainty is honest and large: near-identical rasters in the history differ by up to 0.0856 in reported score, and no organizer receipt can be obtained from this sandbox. Nothing here supports claims near the 0.3195 / 0.4413 / 0.4544 leader band.
