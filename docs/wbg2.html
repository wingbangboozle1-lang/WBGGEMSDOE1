04 / SCIENTIFIC LIMITS

WBG-2 is a spatial prior, not a fault detector

The output samples a smoothed density of already mapped structures.

MECHANISM

Mapped-fault KDE

A 1.5 km Gaussian kernel smooths the visible catalogue; candidate cells inside a 400 m exclusion are removed; a 36,000-cell point field is sampled from the remaining prior. No feature-level evidence for an individual hidden fault is added.

NAMED NON-FAULT MIMIC

Basin geometry

Basin-fill depocentres and range-front alluvial aprons can cluster near mapped faults. A density prior can reproduce sedimentary basin geometry rather than undiscovered fault traces.

WITHDRAWN PROJECTION

Not a score

The nine-row regression used user-reported values, duplicate prediction fields and collinear predictors, then extrapolated outside the fit range. It is retained in the JSON solely for audit and must not be quoted as an estimated score.
